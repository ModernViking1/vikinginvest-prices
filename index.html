The Viking Invest Trading Platform

Designed and built with Claude AI, the Viking Invest Trading Platform helps professional traders anticipate cyclical phases and execute with discipline. Live signal generation, a transparent backtest engine, and an investor-grade performance report sit on the same datastore — what traders act on, investors see. Every edge is validated out-of-sample and reconciled against real broker fills before it earns capital — rigour over backtest theatre.

  • ✓40 live pairs across FX majors & minors, commodities, indices and crypto, plus a US-equity research pilot.
  • ✓Live Viking Edge — multi-timeframe signal engine: a four-layer confluence path plus parallel momentum-cross detectors per asset class. Methodology under NDA.
  • ✓Per-asset-class production routing — macro, execution and risk-sizing tier chosen from each pair's edge curve. One engine, class-aware.
  • ✓Fully automated entry, in-trade and exit; post-trigger noise filters keep clean wins from being reclassified as invalidations.
  • ✓Real-time Performance tab plus on-demand investor-grade PDF (ITD / YTD / MTD / 7-day, allocation, top & bottom pairs, methodology, risk).
  • ✓Live broker bridge to cTrader — server-side dedup, daily-loss limiter, kill-switch, Telegram alerts; broker fills reconcile to the dashboard in real time.
  • ✓12-month backtest engine on institutional-grade price history with traded-volume analysis, auto-refreshed six-hourly, with a per-pair failure-mode panel and trade journal.
  • ✓Out-of-sample & walk-forward validation on every edge — more than 75 published strategies stress-tested; only a small minority survived. Expectancy is measured against real broker fills, so idealised-vs-live is quantified, not hidden — durable edges, not backtest illusions.
  • ✓Live forward-test harness — new candidates run on an isolated demo with model-vs-real tracking before any real capital is committed; now extended to US-equity strategies.
  • ✓Integration-ready — the structured data layer can be delivered with a React front-end and connected to Azure SQL or a client SaaS database, for teams embedding it in their own stack.

Who we are

Where regulatory expertise meets AI engineering

Viking Invest Ltd was founded in 2011 as a business management consultancy specialising in regulatory initiatives and change management for the financial and banking sector. We bring more than twenty years of senior business analysis and delivery from large transformation programmes at Tier-1 investment banks and blue-chip companies.

More recently we expanded into AI trading — designing and building a live trading platform with Claude AI that helps traders anticipate cyclical phases and execute with discipline. The same engineering rigour and focus on project execution now powers our AI consultancy work.
