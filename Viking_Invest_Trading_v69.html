▸ Viking Edge — Entry Rules

① Macro (Daily)

Daily/weekly chart structure sets the macro bias. Only trade in the direction of the dominant trend. Impulsive phases = highest probability; corrective phases set counter-trend caution zones. Must agree with all 4 layers — macro alone is not enough.

② 4H EMA Cloud

4H EMA21 vs EMA55 cloud direction. Bull when EMA21 above EMA55, bear when below. Acts as the medium-term trend filter sitting between the daily macro and the 1H channel reading. Must agree with all 4 layers before any trade fires.

③ 1 Hour

Price oscillates within a bearish or bullish channel on the hourly. Only enter when price is at the channel boundary — resistance (bear) or support (bull). Break of the hourly channel = CHoCH confirmation signal. Mid-channel = stand aside.

④ 15 Minute

After 15m CHoCH confirmed, wait for price to retest the Order Block (OB) or FVG that caused the CHoCH. Limit entry at OB 50% / Fib 38% for fib-class pairs, or wick-extreme for wick-class pairs. Stop beyond swept structural extreme. Target next liquidity zone. Partial signals (1/4, 2/4 or 3/4) are WATCH only — never entered.

Methodology · production rules per pair class
Each pair's headline win-rate & backtest card pull from the cache + entry methodology that performed best in the May 2026 review (struct / auto-EW / +50/200):
  • FX majors except AUD/USD (EUR/USD, GBP/USD, USD/JPY, USD/CAD, USD/CHF, NZD/USD) — STRUCT macro + wick entry, full 1R risk. Structural-break direction outperformed auto-EW by 1.5–6 pts on these pairs.
  • AUD/USD, FX minors, crypto — AUTO-EW macro + wick entry, full 1R risk. Auto-EW edged structural on AUD/USD; the auto-EW pattern detector is the production macro for minors & crypto.
  • Commodities (XAU, XAG, Brent) + indices (DAX, NAS, etc.) — AUTO-EW macro + Fib 38% half-size entry, 0.5R risk. Half-size Fib produced higher net R on these instruments.
Live signals and Telegram alerts follow the same per-class rule (no wick alerts on Brent, no Fib alerts on EUR/USD). The comparison caches are preserved for future re-evaluation.
