Viking Live Book

Every live strategy's causal 3-year backtest (look-ahead removed) set beside its live forward test to date, most profitable on top. Bars are expectancy in R per trade on one zero-centred scale. This is a reconciliation of backtest against live — not a returns claim.

Read it straight. The backtest is causal and positive in both out-of-sample halves. The live forward test is early and thin — most strategies have under ~40 fills — and on the names with enough trades it is running below backtest: the remaining gap is execution (slippage, cost, fill timing), now being closed by the switch to limit entry. The equity book is on MT5 demo; its live column fills in automatically as the first demo trades close. So these backtest figures are not yet confirmed as achievable; real capital waits until live tracks the backtest.
