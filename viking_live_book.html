How the rank works. Score = a blend of live and backtest expectancy (R/trade): the backtest sets the starting line, and each real closed trade pulls the score toward the live mean — so a strategy climbs as it proves itself live, and one lucky or unlucky fill can't catapult it on tiny n. The equity book is MT5 demo; its live fills join automatically as trades close. These figures are not yet confirmed as achievable — real capital waits until live tracks the backtest.
